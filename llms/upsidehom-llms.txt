# Upside (UpsideHoM)

> Upside (joinupside.com, originally UpsideHōM) is a housing-stability company
> for health plans and employers. It finds, houses, and stabilizes members whose
> housing instability disrupts health, care, and outcomes — accepting referrals,
> assessing housing needs, executing stabilization or transitions, coordinating
> with community partners, and documenting outcomes for Medicaid and Medicare
> plans and employer programs. Techstars portfolio company.

Upside publishes no public developer program: no API documentation, developer
portal, OpenAPI specification, SDKs, or /.well-known/ API artifacts were found
on joinupside.com as of 2026-07-21. The website is WordPress and exposes only
the platform's standard /wp-json/ REST surface.
